Tips for getting paid faster

  • Always add a clear due date. Clients pay faster when the deadline is visible.
  • Write each service in one clear line, so the client knows what they are paying for.
  • Double-check your bank or UPI details before sending.
  • Save a copy of every invoice, so your records stay organized.

Frequently asked questions

Is this tool free?
Yes. You can create and print invoices without any signup or payment.
Is my data stored on a server?
No. Your details are saved only in your own browser on this device.
What is the Invoice No.?
A unique number for each bill, such as INV-001. It is created automatically. Click "New invoice" to start the next one.
Can I save the invoice as a PDF?
Yes. Click Print / Save as PDF and choose "Save as PDF" in the print window.
Can I change the currency?
Yes. Type any symbol in the Currency Symbol box, such as $, Rs, €, or £.
